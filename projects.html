Bio-Vision Surgical

A browser-native, gesture-interactive pipeline for patient-specific coronary visualization, running entirely on consumer hardware — no specialized workstation required to explore a patient's cardiac anatomy before surgery.

Zero-Footprint Virtual Dissection

A gesture-controlled DICOM viewer for sterile operating rooms — no touchscreens, no controllers. Built on TypeScript and Electron with MediaPipe hand tracking and Cornerstone.js, driven by a formal state machine for precise, hardware-free navigation on commodity hospital PCs.

TypeScriptElectronMediaPipeCornerstone.js
Poster selected — IEEE EMBC 2026, Toronto
